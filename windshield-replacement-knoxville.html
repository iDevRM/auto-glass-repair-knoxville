What Does Windshield Replacement Involve?

The job starts with covers over the hood, fenders and dash. The technician cuts the old glass free from its urethane bond and trims the old adhesive down to a thin, even layer. Any bare metal on the frame gets primed so rust cannot start under the new seal, which matters on cars that spend the winter on brined roads. Then fresh urethane goes down, the new windshield is set and centered, and the mirror, sensors and moldings go back on. Plan on about an hour, plus cure time.

When Do You Need Windshield Replacement?

A crack longer than a dollar bill

Long cracks cannot be stopped with resin and keep running.

Damage in the driver’s view

Resin leaves a faint mark, so breaks in front of the driver need new glass.

A crack at the edge

Edge cracks weaken the bond between glass and frame and spread fast.

Several chips at once

Three or more chips, common for I-75 and I-40 commuters, add up to weak glass.

A leak at the top corners

Water inside after a hard rain often points to a failed seal.

Pitting that glares at night

Years of grit and winter road treatment leave tiny pits that make headlights flare.

Why Do Windshield Cracks Spread in a Tennessee Winter?

A windshield is two layers of glass with a clear plastic layer in between, and a rock chip breaks the outer layer. Glass shrinks in the cold and expands as it warms, and every swing pulls at the ends of the break. In Knoxville the worst moment is a frosted car on a 25-degree morning with the defroster turned to high. Water that seeps into the chip can freeze and push it wider, and potholes that open up on I-640 after a freeze add a jolt on top. A chip picked up in October is often a crack across the glass by the end of January.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I drive after a new windshield?

Most urethane adhesives reach safe drive-away strength in one to three hours, and cold weather pushes that toward the long end. The technician gives you the exact time before leaving. Driving too soon can let the glass shift or weaken the bond the airbag relies on.

Is OEM glass better than aftermarket glass?

OEM glass comes from the same supplier that made your original windshield. Good aftermarket glass meets the same federal safety rules, but on cars with cameras or heads-up displays OEM glass can make calibration easier. We tell you which options fit your vehicle and your insurance.

When can I wash my car after a windshield replacement?

Wait at least a day before a car wash, and keep pressure washers away from the edges of the glass for a couple of days so the new seal is not disturbed.

Can I use the defroster right after a new windshield?

Yes, once the technician says the car is ready to drive. Start it on a low setting for the first few days instead of blasting high heat at cold glass.
