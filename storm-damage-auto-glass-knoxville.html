What Does Storm Damage Auto Glass Involve?

After a storm, the technician first checks every piece of glass and the frame around it, since a limb that cracks the windshield often dents the roof edge too. Loose glass is cleared and the cabin is vacuumed. Then each broken window or windshield is replaced, the seals are checked for leaks and the forward camera is calibrated if the windshield was changed. Photos taken before the work help with the insurance claim. The question we hear first is “Will insurance cover a tree falling on it?” Usually, yes.

When Do You Need Storm Damage Auto Glass?

A limb on the car

Ice-heavy limbs from oaks, maples and tulip poplars come down in winter storms.

Hail from a spring storm

Hail can star, pit or crack glass across the whole car.

Debris in high wind

Loose branches, shingles and yard items break windows in strong storms.

A window left open to rain

Even a small break lets water soak carpet and electronics.

Cracks that showed up later

Glass flexed by a hit can crack a day or two afterward.

Several windows at once

One visit can handle the windshield and side glass together.

Why Do Ice Storms Break So Much Auto Glass in East Tennessee?

Ice is heavy. A coating of freezing rain can add hundreds of pounds to a large limb, and the hardwoods that shade so many Knoxville streets and driveways were not built to carry that. Cars parked under trees in Sequoyah Hills, Fountain City and the older neighborhoods take the hit when the limbs give way. In spring, thunderstorms coming over the Cumberland Plateau can drop hail. It feels unfair to lose a windshield to a tree in your own yard, but it is one of the most common storm claims we see.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is storm damage to my windshield covered by insurance?

Falling limbs and hail are usually covered under comprehensive coverage, with your regular deductible applying. Liability-only policies do not cover storm damage to your own car.

Should I move my car out from under the tree?

Only if it is safe. Ice-loaded limbs can keep falling for hours, and power lines may be down. Take photos where the car sits first if you can do it safely.

Can you replace the glass if there is also a dent in the roof?

Often yes. Minor dents near the glass do not stop the install. If the frame around the windshield is bent, a body shop needs to fix it first so the new glass seals properly.

How soon after a storm can you come out?

Call as soon as the roads are safe. After a big ice storm or hail event demand spikes, so getting on the schedule early helps.
