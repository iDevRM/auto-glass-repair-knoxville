What Does Windshield Chip Repair Involve?

First the chip gets cleaned and any moisture inside is dried out, which matters in a damp Knoxville winter. A bridge tool is mounted over the damage with suction cups. It pulls the air out of the break, then pushes clear resin in under pressure. A UV lamp cures the resin hard, and the surface is scraped and polished flat. Most chips end up much harder to see, and they stop spreading.

When Do You Need Windshield Chip Repair?

A bullseye or star chip

Round and star-shaped chips under a quarter are the best candidates.

Short crack off a chip

A single crack under about six inches can often still be filled.

A chip before the first freeze

Filling it before frost season keeps ice and the defroster from running it.

Leased car with a chip

A clean repair helps avoid a charge for damaged glass at turn-in.

Fresh hit from the interstate

Stones from trucks on I-40, I-75 and Alcoa Highway are the usual cause.

A chip outside the driver’s view

Repairs leave a faint mark, so they work best away from the driver’s eye line.

Why Does a Small Windshield Chip Turn Into a Crack?

A chip is a break in the outer layer of glass, and every change in temperature stresses its edges. Frost overnight, a hot defroster in the morning and a sunny 60-degree afternoon can all happen in one Knoxville day in March. Water that gets into the chip freezes and expands. Potholes and slammed doors add shocks. People ask, “Can it wait until spring?” Sometimes. Through a cold snap in January, usually not.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a windshield chip repair take?

Most chip repairs take about 30 minutes on site, and you can drive right away. The resin is cured with UV light, so there is no waiting for it to dry.

Will the chip disappear completely?

Usually it gets much harder to see, but a faint mark often stays. The main goal is to stop the chip from spreading and give the glass back its strength.

Should I put tape over a chip until it is fixed?

Clear tape over the chip keeps dirt and water out until the repair. On cold mornings, also start the defroster on low instead of high so the chip does not run.

Can a chip be repaired when it is below freezing?

Yes, but the glass has to be warmed and dried first so the resin flows and cures properly. We bring what is needed to do that, or work in your garage.
